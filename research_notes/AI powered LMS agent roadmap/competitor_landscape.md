# Competitor Landscape: What AI Learning Products Ship (late 2026) and What Differentiates Them

Context: Creators Lab (Next.js + MongoDB LMS, creative-skills video courses, India/INR/Razorpay). Planned agents: Concierge onboarding, RAG Tutor over transcripts, in-video checkpoint quizzes, Examination w/ rubric grading, Performance Report, Study Coach, Revision/flashcards (SRS), Instructor Course Builder, Support, Admin Insights, Project Feedback via vision.

Research note: ~20 searches/fetches. Many 2026 results came from SEO aggregators or vendor blogs. Those are flagged, and primary or credible-press sources are preferred where they exist.

## 1. What concrete AI features does each product ship?

### Takeaway
By 2026 every major platform ships a course-grounded AI tutor or chat. Leaders are now competing on four things: (a) voice and video conversation practice with feedback (Duolingo Video Call, Speak AI Tutor Calls, Udemy and MasterClass roleplay), (b) AI dubbing and translation at catalogue scale (Coursera), (c) instructor-configured Socratic or dialogue activities and AI authoring (Coursera Coach and Course Builder, Udemy Role Play built by creators), and (d) multimodal study artefacts generated from source material (NotebookLM audio and video overviews, quizzes, flashcards). The creative-skills incumbents (Skillshare, Domestika) show no verified native AI tutoring at all.

### Inferences
- Of Creators Lab's planned agents, the RAG Tutor, quizzes, flashcards and Course Builder all have direct analogues at Coursera, Udemy, NotebookLM and YouTube. These are table stakes for a 2026 launch, not differentiators.
- **Missing from the plan vs competitors:**
  1. **Voice or video conversational practice and roleplay** (Duolingo, Speak, Udemy, MasterClass). For creative skills this could be a mock client brief or pitch, a director–DP shot-planning conversation, or a crit session.
  2. **AI dubbing and translation of the video catalogue** (Coursera: voice-preserving and lip-synced, plus 4,000 courses translated into Hindi).
  3. **Instructor-configurable dialogue activities** (Coursera's Socratic activities defined by objectives and rubric). This is a lighter-weight authoring layer than full course building.
  4. **Generated study artefacts** (audio or video recaps à la NotebookLM).
  5. **Career guidance and skills mapping** (Coursera career Coach, Degreed and Docebo skills graphs, LinkedIn auto-posted credentials).
  6. **Visual and interactive explanations** (Khanmigo diagrams).
  7. **Human-in-the-loop review** of AI feedback (the LearnLM Eedi RCT design).
- Project Feedback via vision has no verified analogue at Skillshare, Domestika, Udemy or Coursera for creative work. It is the most clearly differentiating planned agent, since it targets the gap that CG Spectrum and School of Motion fill with paid human mentors.

### Gaps
- No primary source retrieved for the current Udemy AI Assistant feature list, LinkedIn Learning AI Coach, Synthesis Tutor, Brilliant's AI, Adobe or Blackmagic learning AI, Gnomon or Berklee Online AI. These were not verified and should not be claimed.
- Coursera AI grading specifics (rubric peer-review grading, accuracy) were not verified beyond the Braintrust case study.

## 2. Table stakes vs differentiating capabilities

### Takeaway
Table stakes in 2026: a course-grounded chat tutor, Socratic or "don't just give the answer" behaviour, auto-generated quizzes and flashcards, AI-assisted authoring, multi-language subtitles. Differentiating: real-time voice or video tutoring and roleplay with scored feedback, voice-preserving dubbing, AI feedback on learner-produced work, skills graphs tied to careers, and measured learning outcomes.

### Cited Findings
- Socratic tutoring is free in ChatGPT (Study Mode, all plans) and Claude (Learning style) — [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/openai-launches-study-mode-for-chatgpt-while-education-users-are-told-to-wait-and-learn-later/); [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/claudes-new-learning-modes-take-on-chatgpts-study-mode-heres-what-they-do)
- Coach covered 97% of Coursera courses by Q3 2025 (secondary source) — [Simply Wall St](https://simplywall.st/community/narratives/us/consumer-services/nyse-cour/coursera/5iryx0q7-new-courses-and-ai-dubbing-will-expand-global-reach)
- Video Q&A and quizzes on any academic YouTube video come from Google itself — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/05/14/google-ai-generated-quizzes-academic-videos-youtube)
- Duolingo moved Explain My Answer (text explanation) to free tier, while Video Call (voice) stays premium or in test. This signals that text explanation has been commoditised and voice is the monetisable tier — [MarketBeat Q1 2026](https://www.marketbeat.com/earnings/reports/2026-5-4-duolingo-inc-stock/)
- Speak's newest premium-tier feature (Oct 2026) is AI Tutor Calls — [Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/speak-ai-tutor-call-3725246)
- Udemy's 2026 investment is video avatars for Role Play — [StreetInsider](https://www.streetinsider.com/entities/Mark+Zuckerberg/UDMY)
- GenAI features cost money: Duolingo is guiding a ~4-point gross-margin hit — [MarketBeat Q1 2026](https://www.marketbeat.com/earnings/reports/2026-5-4-duolingo-inc-stock/)

### Inferences
- **Table stakes:** RAG Tutor, checkpoint quizzes, flashcards and SRS, Support bot, Course Builder, Performance Report.
- **Differentiating for Creators Lab:** vision-based project critique on actual renders, grades and edits (frame-level and timestamp-level notes, paintover-style annotations); a timestamp-anchored tutor that deep-links back into the lesson; a Hinglish voice tutor; and portfolio and career outputs.
- In-video pause-and-quiz exists (YouTube timed quizzes), so it is not unique. Making the quiz adaptive (generated from the transcript and the learner's weak spots) is a modest edge.
- Unit economics matter. Voice and vision features should be gated to paid tiers, mirroring Duolingo and Speak.

### Gaps
- No vendor publishes comparative data on which AI feature drives conversion. The table-stakes classification is an inference from feature ubiquity and pricing moves.

## 3. Published outcome data

### Takeaway
Outcome data is thin and mostly vendor-reported. The best numbers are Coursera's (+9.5% first-attempt quiz pass rate, +11.6% lessons per hour) and an exploratory LearnLM RCT. Khan Academy candidly reports low adoption (~15%).

### Cited Findings
- Coursera Coach: +9.5% first-attempt quiz pass rate and +11.6% lessons completed per hour, across 1M+ learners — [Coursera blog](https://blog.coursera.org/announcing-ai-powered-capabilities-enabling-educators-to-use-coursera-coach-to-deliver-interactive-personalized-instruction). Q2 2025 commentary tied the tutor plus dubbing to ~10% higher quiz pass rates — [MarketBeat Q2 2025](https://www.marketbeat.com/earnings/reports/2025-7-24-coursera-inc-stock)
- Khanmigo: ~269K weekday interactions and 108M+ total since 2023, but only ~15% of students with access engage. Khan tracks "next-item correctness" (can the student solve the next problem unaided) and reports promising but unpublished early results — [Khan Academy blog](https://blog.khanacademy.org/learning-in-the-open-what-ai-is-and-isnt-changing/)
- LearnLM / Eedi exploratory RCT (Dec 2025): 165 students in 5 UK schools. Human tutors supervised every AI-drafted message and approved 76.4% with zero or minimal edits. A secondhand summary says the AI matched human tutors on immediate success and beat them on short-term transfer — [arXiv 2512.23633](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.23633); [Mr Barton podcast](https://podcast.mrbartonmaths.com/?p=6202)
- Duolingo: words spoken per user in Video Call more than doubled in a year, and CURR retention hit an all-time high in Q2 2026. Making Explain My Answer free did not move DAU much — [MarketBeat Q1 2026](https://www.marketbeat.com/earnings/reports/2026-5-4-duolingo-inc-stock/); [Webull Q2 2026](https://www.webull.com/news/15392596720296960)
- Udemy: 3,100+ creator-made Role Plays in 4 weeks, showing that creator-side supply adoption is fast — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250528283452/en/Udemy-Launches-AI-Powered-Role-Play-to-Coach-Professionals-on-Business-and-Communication-Skills)
- PhysicsWallah: voice "Ask AI" users ask ~3x more queries per session (vendor-linked, unverified), and AI Guru resolves 4.5M doubts per month (company figure) — [CreateWith/ElevenLabs](https://www.createwith.com/tool/elevenlabs/updates/physicswallah-triples-student-engagement-with-elevenlabs-voice-ai-tutor); [NSE filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/NEAPSPW_23032026203839_Intimation_Press_Release_Vishwas_Diwas_Final_Signed.pdf)
- Third-party Khanmigo claims (e.g. "23% retention gain, 12 students") are unverified and should not be used — [kidsaitools](https://www.kidsaitools.com/fr/articles/khanmigo-review-2026)

### Inferences
- Low tutor adoption is the norm (Khan: 15%). Creators Lab should embed AI at moments of need (in-video pause, project submission, failed quiz) rather than rely on an opt-in chat tab.
- Khan's "next-item correctness" and Coursera's first-attempt pass rate are cheap, credible metrics that Admin Insights could adopt from day one.

### Gaps
- No published outcome data on AI feedback for creative or project work, AI dubbing's effect on completion, or AI roleplay learning gains.

## 4. What creative-skills learners value or complain about

### Takeaway
Creative learners pay a premium for feedback on their own work, mentorship and portfolio outcomes. Mass-market creative platforms (Domestika, Skillshare) are criticised for exactly the absence of personalised feedback. Language mismatch is a recurring friction.

### Cited Findings
- Domestika is "not worth it" if you want real-time tutor feedback, certification or a complete beginner grounding (UK review) — [movingtotheuk](https://movingtotheuk.co.uk/education/online-learning/is-domestika-worth-it)
- The Domestika community is active, but teachers respond in forums only "sometimes" (affiliate review) — [wildlifeartstore](https://www.wildlifeartstore.com/is-domestika-worth-it/)
- Domestika has per-course forums but no personalised follow-up or live sessions, and its courses are mostly introductory or intermediate (competitor marketing, biased) — [Coderhouse](https://www.coderhouse.com/en/coderlibrary/que-tal-es-domestika-review-completa-para-creativos-en-espanol)
- A learner complained about a workshop taught in Spanish (language mismatch, single anecdote) — [Goodreads blog](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/19986769-domestika-a-warning)
- Premium creative schools sell human feedback: CG Spectrum offers weekly mentor feedback in classes of 4 plus TA paintovers, and School of Motion offers unlimited feedback, live workshops and portfolio reviews — [Trustpilot CG Spectrum](https://ie.trustpilot.com/review/cgspectrum.com?page=3); [LinkedIn TA](https://cl.linkedin.com/in/mcbork); [School of Motion](https://schoolofmotion.com/courses/next-gen-studio-with-already-been-chewed)
- CG Spectrum reviewers value Slack communities but some want more diverse peer feedback — [Trustpilot](https://es.trustpilot.com/review/cgspectrum.com?page=4)

### Inferences
- Creators Lab's Project Feedback agent should emulate the mentor artefact: annotated frames or paintovers, timestamped notes on edits, colour-scope readings for grading, and an "industry standard" comparison. It should offer an upgrade path to human mentor review (a hybrid tier), which mirrors the LearnLM supervised model.
- Missing from the plan: **community and peer-critique features** (AI-moderated crit threads, AI-summarised peer feedback) and a **portfolio and career layer** (portfolio builder, showreel review, verified skill badges that can be shared to LinkedIn or Behance).

### Gaps
- No Reddit or primary survey data was retrieved on Skillshare learner complaints. No quantitative survey of creative learners' priorities was found.

## 5. Gaps and opportunities for a creative-skills LMS in India

### Takeaway
Indian edtech leaders are already shipping Hinglish voice tutors and vernacular content (PhysicsWallah). Coursera serves India with Hindi translation. No creative-skills platform appears to offer Hinglish or regional-language AI tutoring, dubbing or critique. That space is open but will not stay open.

### Cited Findings
- PhysicsWallah upgraded Ask AI with voice (ElevenLabs) handling native Hinglish. It is building in-house models (Aryabhata, ConceptGuru, Awaaz) trained on Indian STEM data, Hinglish interactions and multilingual teacher voice data — [CreateWith](https://www.createwith.com/tool/elevenlabs/updates/physicswallah-triples-student-engagement-with-elevenlabs-voice-ai-tutor); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/physicswallah-q4-loss-declines-76-yoy-to-%e2%82%b969-cr-revenue-up-51/)
- PW's line-up includes Ask AI (voice-first, English and Hindi, during live classes), AI Sahayak and AI Guru. A full AI tutor is planned for later in 2026, positioned against OpenAI and Google using Indian student behavioural data — [NSE filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/NEAPSPW_23032026203839_Intimation_Press_Release_Vishwas_Diwas_Final_Signed.pdf); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/story/why-physicswallah-believes-it-can-beat-openai-google-in-indias-ai-tutor-race-534212-2026-06-01)
- PW vernacular enrolments are up 100% year on year across 9 languages (company-reported). Its state board and vernacular vertical reached 393K enrolments and was EBITDA-positive in year one — [ScanX](https://scanx.trade/stock-market-news/companies/physicswallah-records-34-lakh-viewers-at-fifth-vishwas-diwas-event-with-strong-ai-initiative-response/35845860); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/physicswallah-q4-loss-declines-76-yoy-to-%e2%82%b969-cr-revenue-up-51/)
- Coursera: India is its #2 market (23.4M learners), with 4,000+ courses AI-translated into Hindi (dated) — [CXOToday](https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/coursera-translates-4000-courses-into-hindi-and-launches-new-ai-features-to-serve-indian-learners/)
- Domestika and Skillshare have no native AI dubbing. Learners rely on browser extensions for translated subtitles — [Immersive Translate](https://immersivetranslate.com/en/video/domestika-course-translator/)

### Inferences
- Opportunities Creators Lab could own:
  1. A Hinglish-first voice tutor and critique ("yeh shot underexposed hai, lift the shadows in the primaries…").
  2. AI dubbing and subtitles into Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali and Marathi, with technical terms like LUT, keyframe and DaVinci node kept in English.
  3. Mobile-first, low-bandwidth modes: audio-only lessons, transcript-first study, NotebookLM-style audio recaps.
  4. Critique for phone-shot work (most Indian creators shoot on phones for Reels and Shorts).
  5. Career outputs tied to the Indian creator economy (freelance brief roleplay, client-pitch practice, pricing in INR).
  6. Low-cost price tiers via Razorpay with AI usage caps.
- Competitive risk: PW and Coursera have the language infrastructure, and general assistants are free. The defensible moat is creative-domain grounding (instructor rubrics, project datasets, timestamped lessons) plus a feedback-on-work loop.

### Gaps
- No data found on Indian creative-skills learners specifically: willingness to pay, language preference, or device and bandwidth profiles. No India-focused creative LMS competitor (e.g. regional filmmaking or design academies) with AI features was researched.
